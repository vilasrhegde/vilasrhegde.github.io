Signal 32

A small sci-fi investigation game. Land on Kepler-23b, scan the ground, keep your oxygen up and recover 32 fragments of one person: the 32 kinds of adventure I keep falling into.

The game needs JavaScript. Here is everything it hides:

Wild

  • Mountain treks. The view is free. The legs are not.
  • Waterfall treks. Slippery rocks, wet socks, zero regrets.
  • Caves. Echo test: passed.
  • Forests. Wi-Fi: none. Signal: excellent.
  • Lakes. Still water, loud thoughts finally quiet.
  • Beaches. Sand in every pocket, forever.
  • Islands. The only way out is a boat. Fine by me.
  • Camping. Sleeping bags: 1. Stars: all of them.
  • Safari. Plot twist: the animals are the main characters.
  • National parks & zoos. Nature's own reality TV.

Roads & ruins

  • Urban exploration. Every alley is a side quest.
  • Bike rides. Two wheels, zero traffic jams in the head.
  • Forts. Walls that have heard centuries of gossip.
  • Temples. Architecture with an extra layer of calm.
  • Sightseeing. Photos taken: many. Photos posted: some.

Thrills & arena

  • Adventure parks. Adrenaline, legally obtained.
  • Stadium. A crowd's roar is the best surround sound.
  • Sports & games. Winning is nice. Playing is nicer.
  • Competition. Gets better every time someone is faster.

Stage & screen

  • Stand-up comedy. Laughed so hard the abs got a workout.
  • Concerts. Phone flashlights: the modern lighter.
  • Movies. Popcorn is a food group during a film.

Mind & universe

  • Science. Curiosity: still undefeated.
  • Physics. Gravity: not just a good idea, it's the law.
  • Reading books. Books started: 12. Books finished: also 12, eventually.
  • Debate. Winning arguments, losing voice.
  • Politics. Reads the manual on how societies run.

Heart & soul

  • Love. Undefined behaviour. Highly recommended.
  • Life. Still in beta. Updates ship daily.
  • Manifestation. Intentions committed, merge pending.
  • Spirituality. Debugging the inner runtime.
  • Cooking. Recipes are just code you can eat.
